# Pocket list

The short list of questions from lesson six — what is worth asking of any course,
school, or circle before giving them your time.

- Who will answer my question about the method — and are they qualified to?
- Where does the method come from? Are its origins named?
- Where are the living people in this format? Who will hear my experience?
- What is done here with the difficult?
- Can I leave — quietly and without consequences?
- How does this end? Does the school build its own unnecessity?

Practice replaces neither a doctor nor a psychotherapist. If something surfaces
that belongs with a specialist, going to one is the road — not a sign of "failure."
