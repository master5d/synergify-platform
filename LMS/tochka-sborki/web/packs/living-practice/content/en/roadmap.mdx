# Course map

Six short lessons, read in order — about an hour of reading time.

1. **The elephant in the room** — what a solitary practice cannot see.
2. **Anatomy of a dead practice** — a checklist of distinctions.
3. **Witnessing** — why feelings need a witness; the boundary "practice is not therapy."
4. **Awareness without sealing over** — understanding yourself in the world vs. repression.
5. **The practice circle** — how to assemble a living format; a bridge to the IGI ritual.
6. **How to choose a school** — the questions a school must answer.

The course promises nothing: no outcome, no state. It is a map of distinctions, not a method.
