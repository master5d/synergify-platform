# Course map

Eight steps, one a week: a short piece of theory, one sitting session of 10–15 minutes, and a conversation about what happened in it.

1. **The agreement: what is here and what is not** — benefit and risk named before the first session; stop signals.
2. **Breath: the anchor you always carry** — why "more" does not mean "better."
3. **Body: a scan with your eyes open** — the window of tolerance and the right to stop.
4. **Thoughts: naming instead of fighting** — a thought as an announcement, not a command.
5. **The uncomfortable: what to do when it surfaces** — difficulty inside the window is material; beyond its edge it is a signal to stop.
6. **Awareness without sealing over** — a practice that does not turn into noise-cancelling headphones.
7. **Talking with AI: an interlocutor, not a curator** — what the companion may and may not do.
8. **The circle: where all of this leads** — how to assemble a living format and how to choose a school.

The course promises nothing: no outcome, no state. Practice does not replace therapy.
