Picture a trip people talk about as “we'll go some day”. For years it stays a conversation. Then someone buys a ticket for a particular date.

**Which of the two trips will happen?** Think about what exactly one ticket changed.

</Phase>

<Phase type="reflection">

Go back to the ticket. It has a date, a place and a name. A wish has become a plan that can be cancelled, but can no longer be forgotten.

**A step without a date stays a conversation.** A step with a date becomes a ticket.

</Phase>

<Phase type="concept">

## What a plan is made of

The plan of the first step gathers everything you did in the course:
- the form of the meeting (lesson 04/u1);
- the date and how many people;
- whom to invite and why (04/u2);
- which steps of the method go into the meeting (02/u3);
- how to tell current clients and who stays one to one (04/u3);
- the sign that the step worked.

## The sign that the step worked

The sign is named before the meeting, not after. For example: “three of four will want a second meeting”. Or: “each will say in their own words what they took away”.

Without a sign, any meeting seems to have gone well. Or badly.

## “My map” on the ladder

The format ladder page has a “My map” block. In “Where I am now”, set the “one to one” step.

In “My next step”, choose “my own format” and write in the name of the meeting. It is not in the list of formats, and that is fine. Add the date and the sign.

The map is kept only in this browser. Copy it or print it so you do not lose it.

## A worked example

The leader's plan: “Small group: breath and back”. Four people, a Thursday evening six weeks from now, 90 minutes.

The meeting includes steps 1, 3, 5 and 6 of her method plus one shared exercise. The sign: three will want a second meeting. The one-to-one sessions stay.

## “When I have the time”

The time will not free up: module 03 showed that the week runs into its limit. **The first step is put into the week, not left waiting for room to appear.**
