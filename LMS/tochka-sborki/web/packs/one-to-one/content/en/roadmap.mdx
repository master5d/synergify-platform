# Course map

The course runs in one line: four modules, and each ends with an output. Each lesson takes about ten minutes, 14 lessons in all.

1. **A map of your practice** — where you stand on the format ladder, signs of a ceiling and of “too early”, what the client gets from the method and from the relationship, the edge of competence. Output: a map of your practice.
2. **A method on one page** — what repeats and what rests on you, the structure of a session, the test on two people. Output: a method on one page.
3. **Hours, price, limit** — the price of a working hour, the cycle and the cancellation rule, the limit of the week. Output: the limit, worked out.
4. **The first step up** — which meeting for 2–7 people, whom to invite, how to tell current clients, the plan and “My map”. Output: the plan of the first step.

One-to-one work is the bottom step of the ladder, but not the worst. A higher step is not better than a lower one, and part of the practice stays one to one. A map of all the steps is the [format ladder](https://academy.synergify.com/en/instrumenty/lesenka/).

The course does not teach the practice itself, promises no income or clients, and does not replace a doctor, a therapist or a lawyer.
