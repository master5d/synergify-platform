<Phase type="concept">

## What the limit is

The limit is the smaller of two numbers. The first is how many client hours a week your body and attention can hold. The second is how many hours are left in the week after life: sleep, the people close to you, rest.

Everyone's limit is their own. You do not borrow it from your neighbours.

## What the data shows

In a 2026 study, burnout among independent practitioners in mental health rose to 42 percent. In 2023 it was 29. One in three cut their workload.

Small qualitative studies of solo private practice name overload with hours and unmanaged workload as precursors of burnout. This is data from a neighbouring profession, but the mechanism is the same.

## The ceiling of the week

The ceiling of the week = the limit of client hours × the price of a session.

The leader's limit is 22 hours. The price is 60. The ceiling is 1320 a week. She dreamed of 30 hours, but her body says “22”.

## How you grow within the step

Within the “one to one” step there are two levers: price and hours. Hours run into the limit.

Price is not endless either. In the same study, 69 percent of practitioners saw clients come less often or stop the work because of the price.

**Above the ceiling you grow not by price and not by hours, but by format.** That is module 04.

The course promises no income. It shows where one format ends and a choice begins.
