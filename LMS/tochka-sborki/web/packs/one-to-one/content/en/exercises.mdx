# Exercises

The practice of each lesson is work with one of five templates. The templates are in the materials section.

## 01 · A map of your practice

- **Where I stand on the ladder** — describe the practice by three axes and fill in the first columns of the map.
- **Signs of a ceiling** — go through the signs of “up” and “too early” and recall a case for each “yes”.
- **Through the client's eyes: what they get** — fill in the “from the method” and “from the relationship” columns for three clients.
- **The edge of your competence** — put together a referral list and fill in the emergency number of your country.

## 02 · A method on one page

- **What repeats and what rests on you** — split your last three sessions into the method and the personal part.
- **The session in steps** — describe a session on one page: action, why, minutes.
- **The test: a method for two** — for each step decide: yes, with a change, or no.

## 03 · Hours, price, limit

- **Hours and the price of an hour** — work out the price of a working hour, counting the work outside sessions.
- **The cycle and regularity** — count the unpaid cancellations and put a cancellation rule into words.
- **Where the limit is** — find your limit of hours and the ceiling of the week.

## 04 · The first step up

- **Which meeting for 2 to 7** — choose the form of the meeting that suits your method.
- **Whom to invite** — draw up a list of three to seven people and three criteria.
- **Through the client's eyes: telling your current clients** — decide who stays one to one and think over the first sentence.
- **The plan and “My map”** — put together the plan with a date and a sign and record the step in “My map”.
