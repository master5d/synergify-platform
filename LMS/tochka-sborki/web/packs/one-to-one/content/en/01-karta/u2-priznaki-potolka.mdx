<Phase type="activation">

Picture a plant in a pot. The leaves are green, it is growing. But the roots reached the walls long ago and circle around the bottom.

**How do you tell the pot has become too small if the leaves look healthy?** Think about what you see from above and what only from below.

</Phase>

<Phase type="reflection">

Go back to the pot. Water runs through the soil faster, new leaves come out smaller. The plant is not sick — it is cramped.

**A ceiling often looks like success.** The practice is full, the clients are pleased, and there is nowhere left for it to grow.

</Phase>

<Phase type="concept">

## Two lists of signs

Every step of the ladder has two lists. The first says “time to step up”. The second says “too early”.

**Time to step up if:**
- people come back to you and bring friends;
- the same questions repeat from person to person;
- there are almost no free hours left in your week.

**Too early if:**
- you cannot yet explain your approach in five minutes;
- after each session you are left with “I don't know what to do with this”.

## What a ceiling is

A ceiling is not “too few clients”. A ceiling is when the signs of “time to step up” are already there, but you cannot take the step.

Usually one of two things gets in the way. Either the practice rests only on you, and it cannot be repeated without you. Or the signs of “too early” are there as well. Then the method comes first (module 02), and the step after it (module 04).

## A worked example

A leader works with the body and with attention. She has 12 regular clients and about 20 hours of sessions a week. Five people are on her waiting list, and two have asked about a group.

The signs of “time to step up” are plain. But she cannot explain her approach in five minutes: “it is different for everyone”. So her ceiling is not time but an undescribed method.

**More clients does not mean growth.** Within the step you can grow only by price or by hours, and both have a limit.

<Phase type="practice">

Go back to your “A map of your practice”.

1. Go through the five signs on the two lists. For each one, decide: do you have it or not.
2. For each “yes”, recall one case from the last month.
3. In the map, mark the signs of a ceiling and of “too early” next to clients, if they show on someone in particular.
4. Look at the result. Signs of “up” and none of “too early”? Both? Neither?

The third option is an honest answer too: then the step is not used up yet.

</Phase>
