## One first clone: five types to choose from

Every understudy follows **Capture (collect examples) → Build (make a first version) → Test (check it) → Refine (tighten it)**: capture an example, build the clone, test it on real work, then refine it from the result.

| Clone | What it does | Saves |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Communication (correspondence)** | Replies to emails in your voice | 2–4 h/day |
| **Meeting Intelligence (meeting summaries)** | Summarizes meetings, writes tasks | 30 min/meeting |
| **Content** | Generates posts, clips, ideas | 5–10 h/week |
| **Learning** | Teaches you new things on a schedule | Quality of life |
| **Automation** | Pipeline: data → report, URL → summary | 5–15 h/week |

The hours in the "Saves" column are the author's rough estimate — your numbers will be your own.

**Build cycle:** Capture → Build → Test → Refine.

> 💡 Build one clone. The five types are a choice map, not a promise to build an army. Modules 06 and 07 show how to build the one you choose.
