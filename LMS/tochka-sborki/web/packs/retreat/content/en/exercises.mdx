# Exercises

The practice of each lesson is a calculation in the academy calculator, a template or a list. The templates are in the materials section.

## 01 · The deal: one retreat from two sides

- **Who is responsible for what** — lay out a retreat across the four contract models and name who pays for empty places.
- **One deal in the calculator** — find the two profits and break-even, compare 20 and 12 guests.
- **First the profit you want, then the price** — add the forgotten lines and derive the price from a target profit.
- **Cash: money over time** — find the cash gap and close it with two levers.

## 02 · Cancellations, a short group, the law

- **Two cancellation calendars** — lay the venue's scale over your terms and remove the gap.
- **"Non-refundable" does not mean untouchable** — rewrite your cancellation terms as a table by days.
- **When a retreat becomes a package holiday** — go through the checklist of signs.
- **Insurance, exchange rate, the body** — sort risks across three insurances, set a currency reserve.

## 03 · Leader: the idea, the guest, sales

- **Who your guest is** — describe one guest in five lines.
- **Your own audience and a co-leader** — estimate the group by your own circle, write down expectations of a co-leader.
- **Sales against the cash plan** — choose a sales pace and payment options in the calculator.
- **Between the first payment and arrival** — place points of contact on the timeline.

## 04 · Leader: the program and the guests

- **An honest practice card** — fill in two cards.
- **The grid of days** — lay out the program with load and rest.
- **The guest form and the menu** — build a menu and carry the cost into the calculator.
- **Arrival logistics** — fill in the arrivals list from a single date.
- **The retreat itself and after** — compare plan with actual and write three terms for next time.

## 05 · Venue: package, occupancy, payback

- **Package and rate** — compare three models through the venue's eyes.
- **Meals in the package** — build a menu and write the final menu deadline into the contract.
- **Occupancy** — work out the venue's year by month.
- **A payback spreadsheet that lies** — find eight mistakes in the teaching model.

## 06 · Venue: leaders and groups

- **Whom to host** — apply the checklist to a teaching leader.
- **Terms from the venue's side** — build a scale from your own obligations.
- **Supporting the group** — place the venue's steps on the timeline.
