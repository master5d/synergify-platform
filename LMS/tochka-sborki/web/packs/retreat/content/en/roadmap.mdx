# Course map

The course starts with a shared core. After that you follow your own path: the leader's or the venue's. Each lesson takes about ten minutes.

## The core — for both roles

1. **The deal: one retreat from two sides** — contract models, one deal in the calculator, a price from profit, cash over time.
2. **Cancellations, a short group, the law** — two cancellation calendars, fair retention, package travel, insurance, the exchange rate.

## The leader path — modules 01, 02, 03, 04 (17 lessons)

3. **The idea, the guest, sales** — who your guest is, your own audience and a co-leader, sales against cash, contact before arrival.
4. **The program and the guests** — the practice card, the grid of days, the form and the menu, logistics, the review after.

## The venue path — modules 01, 02, 05, 06 (15 lessons)

5. **Package, occupancy, payback** — the rate model, meals in the package, the year's occupancy, a payback spreadsheet with mistakes.
6. **Leaders and groups** — whom to host, your own terms, supporting the group.

The course promises no income and no full group and does not replace a lawyer. Legal rules come with a source and a date — "as of 2026-10".
