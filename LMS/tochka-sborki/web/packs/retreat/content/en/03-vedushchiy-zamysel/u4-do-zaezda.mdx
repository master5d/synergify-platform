<Phase type="concept">

## Why guests "cool off"

Months pass between payment and arrival. In that time a person's plans, money and mood change. Without contact from the leader, the retreat turns into a cost line one would rather forget.

Leaders with few cancellations stay in touch all the time. Not selling again, but reminding people what they are going for.

## Points of contact

What works:

- **a personal thank-you** right after payment — a short video or message from you personally;
- **letters about the place:** nature, history, where you will eat, what to bring;
- **materials you already have:** recordings, articles, practices for preparation;
- **a payment reminder** — once every three or four letters, at the bottom, without pressure;
- **a group call** before arrival, so people get to know each other.

## A cancellation is a conversation

A guest goes quiet and does not pay the balance. Often the reason is ordinary: a move, buying a house, a relative's illness. You only learn it in a conversation.

Once the reason is known, you can offer a way out before a refund. A transfer to the next retreat, passing the place to a friend, a different payment schedule. A transfer keeps the money and the guest with you. The transfer rules are written in advance, using the scale from module 02.

## Points of contact are a plan too

Contact with guests is not a mood, it is entries in a calendar. In the timeline template they sit next to the venue payments: [timeline from the start date](/retreat-materials/timeline.en.csv).
