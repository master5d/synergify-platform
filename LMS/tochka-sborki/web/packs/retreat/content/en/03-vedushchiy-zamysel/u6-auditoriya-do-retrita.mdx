<Phase type="concept">

## You sell to your own people

From unit u2: people buy a week from someone they trust. Advertising a retreat to strangers works poorly and eats the profit. So the job before sales is to have more of your own people, and for them to know what you do.

## Teach rather than advertise

The calmest path to trust is to give something useful in advance. A short practice, an article, an open live session, a question taken apart. People try your approach for free and decide whether they want a whole week.

## Steps before the retreat

A retreat is a medium group, in person, one-off: a week for which a person gives both money and holiday time. Before such a step people usually get to know the leader on lower steps — a one-to-one session, a meetup or a breakfast, a weekly group, a masterclass. This is not a status ladder: you can work on any of these steps for years. But that is where your people learn your approach and decide whether they want a whole week. All the formats — from one to one to one to many, in person and online, one-off, regular and cycles — are gathered in the [format ladder](https://academy.synergify.com/en/instrumenty/lesenka/).

## One or two channels

Pick what suits your strength and your character:

- you write — a newsletter, articles, notes;
- you speak — live sessions, a podcast, open classes;
- you connect people — joint meetings with colleagues, mutual recommendations.

A separate path is partners: a colleague, a studio or a venue tells their own people about the retreat. Write the arrangement down in advance: what the partner gets (a percentage, a place on the retreat, an exchange) and how you recognise their guests — by a promo code or by the line "where did you hear about us" in the guest form.

Five channels a little at a time give less than one channel kept regular.

## Rhythm matters more than volume

One idea a week is already a plan. You can live it in several formats: an article on Tuesday, a newsletter on Wednesday, a live session on Thursday, the recording on Friday. That is a promotion calendar: items in the calendar just like venue payments.

## When to start

A new leader needs six to twelve months from the first posts to arrival. The timeline has a milestone "Sales open" — posting starts long before it. If three months are left before it and you have few people of your own, it is more honest to move the retreat than to fill the cash gap with ads (unit u3).

## What is not here

The course does not teach funnels or pressure tactics. The goal is for the people your week suits to hear about it in time.

<Phase type="practice">

1. Write down where your people are now: a newsletter, a social network, a group, clients. Roughly how many.
2. Pick one channel you will keep up regularly. Write down why that one.
3. Make a 12-week promotion calendar: one idea a week and where it goes out.
4. Open your retreat's timeline and note how many weeks before "Sales open" your calendar starts.
5. If there are less than three months between the first posts and sales, write down what you will do: move the dates, make the group smaller or invite a co-leader.

</Phase>
