<Phase type="activation">

Picture a house you return to after a long journey. They meet you at the door, know your name, and the table has what you eat.

**You have not said a word yet, and you were already expected.** Think about what a venue does so that a guest feels the same.

</Phase>

<Phase type="reflection">

Go back to the door where you are met. It is not luxury, it is preparation done in advance.

**Leaders come back to places where the group is met as if at home.** Support is what a venue sells besides beds.

</Phase>

<Phase type="concept">

## One point of contact

The leader should have one person at the venue responsible for their group: before arrival, on site and after. When questions go to different people, the answers diverge.

## Before arrival

- the final guest count and the menu with diets — by the deadline in the contract;
- the arrivals list: who arrives when, whom to meet;
- the hall schedule for the week, following the leader's program grid;
- what the practices need: mats, music, open space.

## On site

- meeting guests at the agreed point;
- the kitchen works from the form: diets and allergies are not lost;
- halls are ready at the start of each practice;
- a short daily check-in with the leader: what to change tomorrow.

## After

A review with the leader after the group: what worked, what did not, which terms to change. It is also the best moment to agree the next dates.

## The venue's timeline

All these steps are entries in a calendar from the arrival date, just as for the leader. Template: [timeline](/retreat-materials/timeline.en.csv). It holds both the payments and the support steps.

</Phase>

<Phase type="practice">

1. Download the [timeline](/retreat-materials/timeline.en.csv) and enter the arrival date of a teaching group.
2. Add the venue's steps before arrival: menu, arrivals list, hall schedule.
3. Add a daily check-in with the leader for the duration of the group.
4. Name one point of contact and their backup in case of illness.
5. Write down three questions for the review with the leader after the group.
