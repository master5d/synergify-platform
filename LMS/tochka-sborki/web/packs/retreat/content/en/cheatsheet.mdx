# Cheat sheet

The numbers are typical of group contracts as of 2026-10. Your numbers are in your contract. This is not legal advice.

## Venue payments

- First payment — 10–25 % of the contract at signing.
- Balance — usually a week to a month before arrival.
- The venue's point of no return — 90–120 days before arrival, after which the charge rises to 100 %.
- Permitted shortfall in hotel contracts — 10–20 % of places.

## Guest terms

- The guests' last refund date comes before the venue's point of no return.
- A small first payment secures the place; when it becomes non-refundable is stated in advance.
- Then a scale by days, tied to your obligations.
- A transfer to the next retreat and passing the place on — as an alternative to a refund.

## Signs of a package holiday (EU and UK)

- There is an overnight stay.
- Accommodation and the program are sold together, at one price or through one link.
- The program is the essence of the offer, or about 25 % of the cost or more.
- The guests are residents of the EU or the UK.

Then the organiser is most likely whoever the guests pay. They need to protect guests' money against their own insolvency.

## Questions for the contract

- Which model: full campus, per guest with a minimum, buyout?
- What does the package include: accommodation, meals, halls, transfers, equipment?
- What is the minimum group and when is it fixed?
- The payment schedule and the cancellation scale by days?
- When is the final guest count and menu due?
- Where are prepayments kept, and what happens if the venue closes?
- What insurance does the venue expect from the leader?
- Which currency is the contract in?
