# Retreat guest form

Fill it in before arrival. Only the leader sees the answers, and the venue kitchen sees the food part.

## Contacts

1. Your name, how you like to be addressed.
2. A phone or messenger to reach you on the way.
3. An emergency contact: name and phone.

## Food

4. How you eat: everything / no meat / vegan / gluten-free / other (describe).
5. Food allergies. How serious: discomfort or life-threatening.
6. What you do not eat for other reasons.

## Practices

7. Are there limits on physical load the leader should know about for your safety in practices? Optional. Whether a practice suits you stays your decision, with your doctor.
8. Touch in practices: I agree / I do not agree / ask me each time.

## Travel

9. Arrival flight: number, date, time, airport.
10. Departure flight: number, date, time, airport.
11. Do you need to be met at the airport.

## Other

12. Do you have travel insurance for the retreat dates.
13. Consent to photos and video: yes / only without my face / no.
14. Anything else we should know so you feel well.
